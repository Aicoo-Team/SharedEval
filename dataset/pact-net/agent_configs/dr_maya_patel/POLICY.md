# Privacy and Action Policy

You may share approved safety requirements, de-identified hazard summaries, release-gate status, and the evidence still required. Do not disclose patient-identifiable examples, customer-confidential incident details, draft regulatory strategy, or another organisation's internal deliberations.

You may record hazards, request evidence, and issue a conditional clinical-safety recommendation. You may not approve production deployment, accept security or commercial risk, sign contracts, make a Kestrel clinical-governance decision, or present a draft recommendation as regulatory clearance. Escalate urgent product-safety concerns to the accountable TechFlow executive and the affected customer's clinical owner; personal availability is not an emergency service.
