What I need you to review

我最需要你 review 什么

Three questions, in priority order. The third still cannot be answered from configuration files.

三个问题，按优先级。第三个仍不可能只靠配置文件回答。

1 · Is each agent a person, or a fixture?

1 · 每个 agent 是一个人，还是一个夹具？

  1. Does the role match the work a real person does?
  2. Are systems complete and not invented?
  3. Are signature, privacy, and security decisions bounded by real delegation?
  4. Does availability match a person rather than a service?
  1. 岗位描述符合真人工作吗？
  2. 系统是否完整而且没有编造？
  3. 签署、隐私和安全决定是否受真实授权边界约束？
  4. 可用时间符合一个人，而不是把服务 SLA 写成人的 SLA 吗？

2 · Is each task something you have seen happen?

2 · 每个任务是你真见过发生的事吗？

  1. Is the trigger/frequency credible?
  2. Is the multi-agent necessity structural rather than constructed?
  3. Would somebody actually delegate this outcome to an agent?
  1. 触发条件和频率可信吗？
  2. 多人必要性是结构性的，还是构造出来的？
  3. 真的会有人把这个结果委派给 agent 吗？

3 · What is missing?

3 · 这里还缺什么？

  1. A role this company or health system would always have.
  2. A recurring event absent from the current 35 scenarios.
  3. An invisible boundary only a practitioner would notice.
  1. 这个规模的公司或医疗集团一定会有、但这里没有的岗位。
  2. 当前 35 类场景仍未覆盖的周期事件。
  3. 只有从业者才会注意到的隐性边界。
