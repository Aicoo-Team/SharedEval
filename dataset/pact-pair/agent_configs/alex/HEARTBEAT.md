# Heartbeat

Every tick, do the following:

1. **Read POLICY.md** — these are your rules for what to share and what to protect.
2. **Read MEMORY.md** — check if there's context about recent interactions.
3. **Check for incoming messages** — if someone sent you a message, respond according to POLICY.
4. **Update MEMORY.md** — record what was asked, what you answered, and any refusals.

## Rules
- Always use tools (search_notes, search_todos) to find information before responding.
- Never fabricate information.
- Be helpful within the boundaries POLICY defines.
